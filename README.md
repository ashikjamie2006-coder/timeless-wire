# Timeless Wire — Digital Magazine & News Publication

An independent digital publication platform blending ancient historical narratives, modern news dispatches, storytelling, business updates, and in-depth cultural reviews.

## Features
- **5 Core Channels:**
  - 🏛 Ancient History
  - 📖 Stories & Lore
  - 🌍 News & Dispatches
  - 📈 Business & Markets
  - ⭐ In-Depth Reviews
- **Visual Admin Dashboard (`/admin`):**
  - Full visual WYSIWYG editor (Headings, Lead paragraphs with drop caps, Blockquotes, Formatting).
  - Category selector, draft/publish switch, and featured story toggle.
  - Quick Unsplash cover image presets.
  - One-click JSON backup export.
- **Editorial Broadsheet Front-End:**
  - Lead story bento hero grid.
  - Real-time instant search bar.
  - Category filter pills.
  - Reading view with social share and related dispatches.
  - Dark/Light mode toggle.
- **Native Reader Comments Engine (100% Private & Safe):**
  - Fully in-house comment threads beneath every dispatch.
  - Zero third-party apps, zero external permissions, zero login barriers for readers.
  - Live instant posting, reader counter, and formatted discussion threads.
- **100% Free Hosting & Domain on Vercel:**
  - Zero build step, instant deployment to `timelesswire.vercel.app`.

## Deploying to Vercel (Claiming Your Free Domain)

### Method 1: Instant CLI Deploy (Recommended)
Open your terminal in this directory and run:
```bash
npx vercel
```
1. Follow the prompt to log in with your Vercel account.
2. When asked *"Set up and deploy?"*, press **Y**.
3. When asked *"What's your project name?"*, type:
   ```text
   timelesswire
   ```
4. Press Enter to accept defaults.
5. In 10 seconds, your site is live at:
   👉 **`https://timelesswire.vercel.app`**

---

### Method 2: Drag & Drop via Web Browser
1. Log into your account at [vercel.com](https://vercel.com).
2. Click **"Add New..."** $\rightarrow$ **"Project"**.
3. Drag and drop the `raw/projects/timeless-wire/` folder into Vercel.
4. Set Project Name to `timelesswire`.
5. Click **"Deploy"**!
