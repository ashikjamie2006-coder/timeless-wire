Legal & Data Ethics

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective: October 5, 2026. Our local-first covenant with readers: zero surveillance advertising, zero third-party tracking cookies.

🛡️
The Timeless Wire Privacy Guarantee: We do not track you across the web. We do not maintain user surveillance databases, fingerprint devices, or sell data to advertisers. Your reading history and bookmarks reside entirely in your personal web browser.

1. Local Storage Architecture

Timeless Wire utilizes your browser's native localStorage to deliver personalization without cloud tracking:

  • Reading Bookmarks: Saved article IDs so you can resume stories later in your Archive Drawer.
  • Reading Log: Timestamps of articles you opened to mark them as ✓ Read on your local screen.
  • Atmospheric Preferences: Your selected reading atmosphere (Parchment, Sepia, or Midnight) and typography size choices.
  • Editorial Passcode & Drafts: Editorial drafts and PIN credentials for the newsroom desk stay isolated in browser sandbox storage.

None of these records are transmitted to an external database. You can clear this data at any moment by clicking "Clear Current Tab" in the Archive Drawer or clearing your browser cookies/storage.

2. Cookies & Third-Party Trackers

Timeless Wire has zero third-party advertising cookies. We do not integrate surveillance pixels from ad networks or cross-site tracking brokers. Our static delivery through Vercel provides basic edge-level DDoS protection and server access logging (such as anonymized IP addresses and HTTP response codes) strictly to ensure site uptime and security.

3. Gazette Subscriptions & Reader Inquiries

If you subscribe to The Timeless Wire Gazette or submit a letter to our editors, we store your email address solely to send dispatches or editorial correspondence. We will never sell, rent, or trade your contact details with external marketers. You may unsubscribe or request data deletion at any time.

4. GDPR & CCPA Rights

Because our core application operates locally in your client environment, you maintain complete sovereignty over your data under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You have the right to access, rectify, export, or delete any data stored locally or submitted via our contact forms.
